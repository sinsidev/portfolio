I got into my first Dungens and Dragons session this year. Needless to say, I enjoyed it a lot.

Then I decided to put some work into creating my first DnD character, which is a wizard specialized in evocation and abjuration.

While I was brainstorming ideias with prompt engeneering and AI image generation, I found something that I liked, and used it for practice study. Then my actual painting and value work has been devalued because it used an AI image for reference.

Which is honestly not understandable. While the reference image itself is as creative as a piece of rock, the actual work into reading values, drawing, proportioning and painting in a whole is still a creative process. It is as much human work as copying something from a painter, or a piece of concrete on the street. It's also not like I'm selling it or being dishonest with the reference images.

Is AI data-scrapping unethical, in general? I believe so, but to say that it is therefore less valuable, or less effortful, human work of the creative process would be a strech. It seems more like a luddite thing to me, kind like the general devaluing of digital work by traditional artists I've seen on the internet (which didn't die yet).
